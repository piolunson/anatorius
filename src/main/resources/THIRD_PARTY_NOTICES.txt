Anatorius bundles Baritone (https://github.com/cabaletta/baritone) by leijurv and Brady,
licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License v3.0 (LGPL-3.0).
It is included unmodified as its own Fabric mod in META-INF/jars/baritone-api-fabric-1.17.0.jar
and can be replaced with any compatible Baritone build. Source: https://github.com/cabaletta/baritone
License text: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/lgpl-3.0.txt

Anatorius includes the client part of CustomSkyboxes by cotii (sky, sun, moon, stars, clouds and
weather replacement, /customskyboxes command), integrated with the author's permission.
